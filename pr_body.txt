## What & why
This PR implements all 6 phases of the PrivGuard DB Hardening plan to ensure database integrity, implement a robust two-phase budget protocol, push differential privacy bounds and aggregations down to PostgreSQL, and harden overall infrastructure. This addresses multiple security, performance, and durability gaps.
